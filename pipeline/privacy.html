Privacy Policy

Book Pipeline is a private, single-user tool. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive, limited in practice to two folders used for e-book files ("Inbox" and "Rakuten Kobo").

What it does with that data

Files are copied to the owner's own computer and e-reader. Nothing is sent to any other person, company or server. No data is collected, shared, sold, or used for advertising, analytics or profiling, and no third party receives Google user data.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
